Costs for each user

One AI action is about RM 0.005 on a low-cost model (DeepSeek class) and about RM 0.08 on a premium one (Claude Sonnet). Servers are about RM 0.60–5.50 per user (see the scaling plan).

Payments and tax

SST is collected from customers and paid to the government, so it is not your income. SME rates: 15% on the first RM 150,000 of yearly profit, 17% up to RM 600,000, 24% above (for qualifying small companies). Confirm both with your accountant.

Fixed costs each month

Salaries should include the employer's EPF, SOCSO and EIS.

Goals, churn and winning customers

The forecast adds the cost to win each new agency (sales, demos, ads, referral rewards) on top of the fixed costs. Leave marketing out of the fixed costs if you count it here, so it is not counted twice.
